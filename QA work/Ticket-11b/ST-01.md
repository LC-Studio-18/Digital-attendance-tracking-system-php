## Information

## Objective
- Verify that `backend/services/QrTokenService.php` issues 64-character hex tokens, formats redirect URLs (`<app-url>/clock-in?qrToken=...`), validates active unexpired tokens with `site_id` and `qr_code_id`, and rejects expired, inactive, malformed, or unknown tokens with `EXPIRED_QR` / `INVALID_QR`.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Token issuer generates 64-character hex tokens and valid redirect URLs.
- [x] Validator enforces active status (`is_active`), UTC expiration (`expires_at_utc`), malformed/unknown checks, and returns `site_id` and `qr_code_id`.
