## Objective
- Verify that users can reach the QR check-in screen directly in-app or via a scanned QR deep link/URL, and that the camera scanner cleanly requests permissions and renders helpful fallback instructions when permission is denied or no camera is detected.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Users can access the QR check-in flow via the designated in-app scanner screen and/or by landing on the check-in route from a scanned QR URL (`frontend/check-in.php`, `frontend/assets/js/check-in.js`).
- [x] Camera permission and scanner fallback states (`permission-denied`, `insecure-context`, `unsupported`, `unsupported-format`, manual entry fallback) are handled cleanly.
