## Objective
- Verify that the status badge implements all four states (`IN`, `OUT`, `Checking...`, and `Status unavailable`) with distinct icons, labels, `aria-live` announcements, and optional detail/since text without guessing status when unconfigured or invalid.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Badge supports `IN`, `OUT`, `Checking...`, and `Status unavailable` states in markup, CSS, and JS.
- [x] Each state includes a non-color SVG icon, clear label, and defaults to `Status unavailable` when endpoint is missing or payload is invalid.
