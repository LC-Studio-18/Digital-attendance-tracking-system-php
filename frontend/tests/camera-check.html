    Mobile checklist

    1. Open this page on your phone over HTTPS (or via localhost on the device).
    2. Confirm the environment facts: secure context yes, BarcodeDetector available.
    3. Tap Start camera and accept the permission prompt; the rear camera should be used.
    4. Point the phone at any QR code; the decoded value should appear under "Last decoded code".
    5. Tap Stop camera and confirm the camera indicator turns off.
    6. Revoke camera permission in browser settings, reload, then start again: the log should show permission-denied.
    7. Open the real check-in screen and confirm manual entry appears when the camera is blocked.
